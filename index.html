Why does skin color vary?
It's all about UV.

Human skin pigmentation is an evolutionary adaptation to UV radiation, not a marker of race. Click any country to explore the science behind the geographic gradient.

ⓘ

Colors show UV intensity by latitude, which shaped ancestral skin pigmentation over thousands of years of evolution. Modern countries are highly diverse due to migration and do not reflect a single skin tone.

Equator — intense UV · darker ancestral pigmentation Poles — minimal UV · lighter ancestral pigmentation
